# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis | Excel

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing over **149,000 coffee shop transactions** using Microsoft Excel to evaluate sales performance, identify customer purchasing patterns, and uncover business insights across multiple store locations, product categories, and time periods.

The goal was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful insights through data cleaning, KPI development, pivot tables, and an interactive dashboard.

## Objectives

- Analyze revenue and overall sales performance.
- Identify the best-performing product categories and product types.
- Evaluate sales trends by month, day of the week, and hour.
- Compare performance across store locations.
- Identify peak business hours and customer purchasing patterns.

## Tools and Skills

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Data transformation and calculated fields
- Pivot tables and data aggregation
- KPI development
- Data visualization
- Interactive dashboard design
- Business intelligence and exploratory data analysis

## Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared and structured to support consistent and reliable analysis. Key tasks included:

- Reviewing data quality and standardizing date and time formats.
- Checking records for inconsistencies and potential duplicates.
- Organizing product categories and transaction attributes.
- Calculating revenue using transaction quantity and unit price.
- Extracting time-based attributes, including year, month, weekday, and hour.

## Key Analysis Areas

### Sales Performance
Evaluated revenue patterns and sales distribution across different periods.

### Product Analysis
Compared product categories and types to understand their contribution to sales.

### Store Performance
Analyzed differences in sales across store locations.

### Time-Based Trends
Examined monthly performance, weekday patterns, and hourly sales activity to identify peak demand periods.

## Dashboard

The interactive Excel dashboard brings the main findings together through visualizations, summary metrics, pivot tables, and filters, allowing users to explore sales performance from different perspectives.
